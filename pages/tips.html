Market Tips

These tips help buyers, farmers, and vendors get the most value from the market.

Tips for Buyers

✅ Always check prices on this website before going to the market so you know the fair price.

✅ Go to the market early in the morning — prices are often lower and products are fresher.

✅ Compare prices from different vendors before buying.

✅ Buy in bulk when prices are low to save money.

✅ Bring a bag or basket to carry your items and avoid plastic waste.

✅ Be polite when bargaining — a good relationship with vendors leads to better deals.

Tips for Farmers and Vendors

✅ Update your prices on this platform regularly so buyers can trust the information.

✅ Sell your products early in the morning for maximum buyers and better prices.

✅ Keep your products clean and well organized to attract more customers.

✅ Group your products by quality and price them accordingly.

✅ Work together with other farmers to share transport costs and reduce losses.

✅ Check what products are in high demand before deciding what to grow or sell.

Tips for Market Leaders

✅ Update the price list on this website every morning before the market opens.

✅ Encourage vendors in your market to register and submit accurate prices.

✅ Report any dishonest pricing to help maintain a fair market environment.

✅ Share this website with people in your community so more people benefit.
