Welcome to Rwanda Market Price Checker

This website helps you find current prices of everyday products in Rwandan markets. Buyers can check prices before going to the market. Vendors can submit prices using the Update Prices page.

About This Platform

The Smart Local Market Price Checker helps people in Rwanda easily find current prices of everyday market products such as beans, rice, potatoes, and vegetables. Vendors and market leaders update prices regularly so buyers always have accurate information. This helps buyers avoid overpaying and helps farmers make better selling decisions.

The application is built to be easy to use, fast, and accessible — especially for people with basic digital skills. Our goal is to improve transparency, save time, and create a more organized and fair market environment for everyone.

What You Can Do Here

  • Check today's prices of market products
  • Find which markets sell what you need
  • Submit or update prices if you are a vendor
  • Read tips on how to buy and sell wisely
  • Get answers to common questions
