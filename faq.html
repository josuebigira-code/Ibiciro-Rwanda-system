Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to common questions about this platform.

What is Rwanda Market Price Checker?

It is a website that shows current prices of everyday products sold in Rwandan markets. It helps buyers know the fair price before going to the market and helps vendors share their prices publicly.

Who can use this website?

Anyone can use this website — buyers, farmers, vendors, market leaders, students, and researchers. It is free and open to everyone.

How often are prices updated?

Prices are updated daily by vendors and market leaders. We recommend checking the date shown on the prices page to know when prices were last updated.

Can I submit prices if I am a vendor?

Yes. Go to the Update Prices page, fill in the form with your name, market, product, and price, then click Submit. Your price will be reviewed and published.

Are the prices exactly the same at every market?

No. Prices may vary slightly between markets depending on location, season, and supply. The prices shown are a general guide based on the most recent updates from vendors.

Is this website free to use?

Yes. This website is completely free for everyone to use. There are no fees or registration required to check prices.

How can I report a wrong price?

You can contact us using the Contact page. Send us the product name, market, and the price you saw, and we will review and correct it as soon as possible.
